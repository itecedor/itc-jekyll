June 2025 -- I collaborated with the shop owner, Christie, to build a new Shopify theme implementing the new <a href="https://themes.shopify.com/collections/horizon-themes" target="_blank">Shopify Horizons</a> functionality, to bring her theme up to modern standards for 2025.

I used custom <a href="https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/custom-data/metafields" target="_blank">product metafields</a> to create structured product data:

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/product-metafields.jpg">

which can be used when browsing products to make it easier for customers to find what they're looking for:

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image/product-filtering.jpg">
