2004-2025 - With my business partner Andrea Deal, I co-founded and ran Gotham Quilts in midtown Manhattan, and online, for over 10 years. We sold fabric and sewing machines, taught classes, and provided longarm quilting services.

In 2025, Gotham Quilts was aquired and continues to serve quilters under new ownership.

Over the years we were in business, I built 3 different versions of our ecommerce site: first using WooCommerce, then with Magento, and finally as a Shopify site.
